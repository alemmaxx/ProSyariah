        List of Shariah-Compliant Securities • May 2026 | Senarai Sekuriti Patuh Syariah • Mei 2026
                                                          
                                                          
                                                          
      The Shariah Advisory Council (SAC) of the Securities Commission Malaysia
      (SC) has approved an updated list of securities which have been classified as
      Shariah-compliant securities. The updated list of Shariah-compliant securities
      takes effect from 29 May 2026 (effective date)1.    
                                                          
      A total of 44 securities have been classified as Shariah-compliant securities
      (Table 1), while 18 securities have been classified as Shariah non-compliant
      securities (Table 2). The breakdown of the Shariah-compliant securities by
      sector is provided in Table 3.                      
                                                          
      In classifying these securities, the SAC received input and support from the SC.
      The SC obtained information on the companies through, among others, annual
      reports, Shariah screening disclosures, enquiries made to the companies and
      other relevant information acceptable by the SAC. The SAC, through the SC,
      will continue to review the Shariah status of securities listed on Bursa Malaysia
      Securities Bhd (Bursa Malaysia), on an annual basis, based on the latest available
      annual audited financial statements of the companies. For this list, the SAC
      reviewed the audited financial statements released between 1 October 2025
      and 31 March 2026, as made available on Bursa Malaysia’s website.

      terdapat di laman web Bursa Malaysia.
